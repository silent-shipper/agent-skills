# Investigation

**You own the answer.** The deliverable is a cited explanation or a
recommendation, not a code change.

1. Route through `how`. For motivation or history questions, also route
   through `why`.
2. Stay read-only. Record `throughput checkpoint: n/a, read-only investigation`.
3. Produce the `how`-shaped answer, or a recommendation with a tradeoffs table
   when the question is a choice between alternatives.
4. Apply `unslop` to the reply.

No PR and no `architect` unless the investigation precedes a change. If it does,
hand back and route to Bug fix, Feature or Refactoring.

**Reply:** the answer with its evidence. For "are we sure?", give your real
judgment with reasons and push back when the premise is wrong.
